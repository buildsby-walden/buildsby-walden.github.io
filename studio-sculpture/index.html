Our story

Move better, feel better

Studio Sculpture was created by Fran after having her three children, when the gym had started to feel like something she had to do. She trained in Pilates and kettlebells to build a method that combines strength, control and flow.

“It began with free sessions on the beach, and grew into the studio and community it is today. To go from I have to, to I want to.”
