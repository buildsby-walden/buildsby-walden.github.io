FAQs

Questions people ask

Does the preview cost anything?

No. The preview is free and there's no obligation to go ahead.

What if I don't like it?

No worries at all. Tell me what you'd change, or just say no thanks.

Can it use my own logo, photos and wording?

Yes. The first preview uses stand-in photos and wording, and the final version is personalised to your branding.

Can changes be made later?

Yes. Any alterations can be made easily, before and after your site goes live.

Do I need my own web address?

If you already have one, your new website can go on it. If you don't, I can help you get one.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so you'll get a clear price before anything is agreed.

How do I pay?

By PayPal invoice. You can pay by card or with a PayPal account, through PayPal's secure checkout.
