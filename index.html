Timetable and booking

Clients see what's on, book in a couple of taps and cancel when plans change. Full classes have a waitlist that moves people in when a space opens.

Passes and memberships

Pay as you go, class passes and monthly memberships, set up with your own prices. Credits come off automatically when someone books.

FAQs

Questions people ask

Does the first version cost anything?

No. I make a working version of your app for free, and there's no obligation to go ahead.

Do my clients have to download anything?

No. It opens from a link on their phone, and they can add it to their home screen like any other app. It can also go on the App Store and Google Play under your studio's name.

I already use a booking system. Can I switch?

Yes. Send me your timetable, prices, passes and memberships, and I'll set them up in your app. I'll help you plan the change so your clients aren't left confused.

Does it take a cut of my bookings?

No. You pay a monthly fee for the app, not a percentage of what your clients spend.

Can it use my own logo, photos and wording?

Yes. The first version uses what's already out there about your studio, and the final version is set up with your own branding, classes and prices.

Can changes be made later?

Yes. New classes, new prices or a new timetable: tell me and I'll change it, before and after it goes live.

How much does it cost?

You'll get a clear price before anything is agreed. It's a monthly fee that covers the app, hosting, the app store listings, support and changes, with the option of a set-up fee for a lower monthly price.

What if I don't like it?

No worries at all. Tell me what you'd change, or just say no thanks.

Do you still make websites?

Yes. If you'd like a website as well as your app, or a "Book now" button on the website you already have, just ask.

Who runs Builds by Walden?

Me, Jay. I work with studios across the UK, online, and you deal with me from the first version to the finished app.

How do I pay?

By PayPal invoice. You can pay by card or with a PayPal account, through PayPal's secure checkout.
