How I use business details

I'm Jay, and I run Builds by Walden. I build websites for small businesses. This page explains what information I use when I contact a business, where it comes from, and how to stop hearing from me.

Don't want to hear from me? Reply STOP to any message, or email jay@buildsbywalden.co.uk. I'll add you to my do-not-contact list straight away and won't contact you again.

What I use and where it comes from

  • Your public business listing on Google Maps: your business name, address, phone number, website link, opening hours, rating and reviews.
  • Your own website, if you have one: what you offer, and whether things like online booking or a menu are on it. I only read pages your website allows automated tools to read.
  • Photos on your public listing: I look at these to match your colours and logo. I never put your Google photos on a website I make for you.
  • Anything you send me yourself, such as replies, your logo or photos.

Why I use it

To see whether a new website could help your business, to make you a free concept to look at, and to contact you about it. I do this because it's in my legitimate interest as a small business offering a service to other businesses, and I keep it to what's needed for that.

I only send marketing emails where the rules allow it: to limited companies (who can opt out at any time), or to people who have agreed to hear from me. I only message people on WhatsApp after they've agreed to it or messaged me first.

Concept websites and presentations

Concept websites and presentations I make for a business are private pages. They're hidden from Google and only people with the link can see them. Each one says clearly that it's a concept made by Builds by Walden, not the business's official website. If you'd like yours taken down, just ask and I'll remove it.

How long I keep it

  • Reviews, ratings and other details from Google listings are deleted within 30 days. If I've made you a concept website, it keeps your business name and contact details until you ask me to take it down or it's no longer needed.
  • If you ask me not to contact you, I keep just enough (your business name and contact details) on my do-not-contact list to make sure I never contact you again.
  • If you become a customer, I keep what's needed to look after your website and for my accounts.

Who else sees it

I don't sell or share your details. I use a small number of services to run my business: Google Workspace for email, and GitHub Pages to host concept websites and presentations. They only process data on my behalf.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it or delete it, or object to me using it, by emailing jay@buildsbywalden.co.uk. If you're not happy with how I've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
